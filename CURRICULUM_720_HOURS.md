# 📜 NIT LONG BEACH: 720-HOUR MECHANICAL ENGINEERING MASTER SYLLABUS

*Accreditation Standard: ABET / NCCER / ASME Polytechnic Framework*  
*Total Instructional Duration: 720 Contact Hours (180 Lab / 360 Machining / 180 Design)*

---

## 🏗️ BLOCK 1: STATICS, DYNAMICS & MATERIALS (HOURS 001 – 120)
* **Hours 001–030:** Vector Statics, Free-Body Diagrams (FBDs), Truss & Frame Equilibrium (${\sum F = 0, \sum M = 0}$).
* **Hours 031–060:** Mechanics of Deformable Bodies: Stress-Strain Tensors, Young’s Modulus ($E$), Poisson’s Ratio ($\nu$), Shear & Bending Moment Diagrams ($V(x), M(x)$).
* **Hours 061–090:** Dynamic Kinematics & Kinetics: Planar Rigid Body Motion, Coriolis Acceleration, Work-Energy & Impulse-Momentum Principles.
* **Hours 091–120:** Precision Metrology: Vernier Calipers, Micrometers, Height Gages, Dial Indicators, Optical Comparators, and Coordinate Measuring Machines (CMM) to $\pm 0.00005\text{ in}$.

---

## ⚙️ BLOCK 2: SUBTRACTIVE MACHINING & 5-AXIS CNC (HOURS 121 – 240)
* **Hours 121–150:** Manual Lathe Operations: Turning, Facing, Taper Turning, Single-Point Threading (60° UN, Acme, Buttress), Boring, and Knurling.
* **Hours 151–180:** Manual Knee Mill Operations: Squaring Stock, Slotting, Flycutting, Boring Head Setup, Precision Tramming.
* **Hours 181–210:** CNC G-Code & Conversational Programming: Modal G-Codes (`G00`, `G01`, `G02`, `G03`), Canned Cycles (`G81`, `G83`, `G76`), Work Coordinate Offsets (`G54`–`G59`).
* **Hours 211–240:** Simultaneous 5-Axis Machining: Dynamic Work Offsets (`G54.2`), Tool Center Point Control (`TCPC / G43.4`), Volumetric High-Speed Trochoidal Milling.

---

## 🔥 BLOCK 3: THERMODYNAMICS & INDUSTRIAL PIPING (HOURS 241 – 360)
* **Hours 241–270:** First & Second Laws of Thermodynamics, Carnot Efficiency, Enthalpy-Entropy ($h-s$) Mollier Diagrams, Rankine Vapor Power Cycles.
* **Hours 271–300:** Fluid Mechanics: Navier-Stokes Equations, Laminar vs Turbulent Flow ($Re$), Darcy-Weisbach Friction Factor, Minor Losses in Valves & Fittings.
* **Hours 301–330:** Industrial Boilers & Steam Traps: High-Pressure Watertube Boilers, Superheaters, Economizers, Inverted Bucket & Thermodynamic Steam Traps.
* **Hours 331–360:** ASME B31.3 Chemical Process Piping: Pipe Schedule Calculations, Flange Ratings (Class 150–2500), Thermal Expansion Loop Sizing.

---

## ⚡ BLOCK 4: FOUNDRY, METALLURGY & STRUCTURAL WELDING (HOURS 361 – 480)
* **Hours 361–390:** Ferrous & Non-Ferrous Physical Metallurgy: Iron-Carbon Phase Diagrams, Austenite/Martensite Transformation, TTT Diagrams.
* **Hours 391–420:** Metal Casting & Patternmaking: Greensand Molding, Gating & Riser Sizing (Chvorinov’s Rule), Investment Lost-Wax Aerospace Pouring.
* **Hours 421–450:** SMAW (Shielded Metal Arc Welding): E6010 Deep-Penetration Root Passes, E7018 Low-Hydrogen Fill & Cap in 3G/4G Positions.
* **Hours 451–480:** GTAW (TIG) & Exotic Alloys: High-Frequency AC TIG on 6061-T6 Aluminum, DCEN Argon Purge Welding on Grade 5 Titanium ($Ti\text{-}6Al\text{-}4V$) and Inconel 718.

---

## 🤖 BLOCK 5: MECHATRONICS, HYDRAULICS & PLC CONTROL (HOURS 481 – 600)
* **Hours 481–510:** Industrial Fluid Power: Fixed vs Variable Displacement Piston Pumps, Proportional Directional Valves, Pilot-Operated Check Valves at 5,000 PSI.
* **Hours 511–540:** Electro-Pneumatics: Valve Manifolds, Rodless Cylinders, Vacuum Venturi Ejectors, Fieldbus Sockets (IO-Link / EtherCAT).
* **Hours 541–570:** Programmable Logic Controllers (PLCs): IEC 61131-3 Ladder Logic & Structured Text on Siemens S7-1500 and Allen-Bradley GuardLogix.
* **Hours 571–600:** Industrial 6-Axis Robotics: Forward & Inverse Kinematics, Denavit-Hartenberg Parameters, Cartesian Singularities, Robot-to-Machine Interlocking.

---

## 🚀 BLOCK 6: TURBINES, AEROSPACE & CAPSTONE SYNTHESIS (HOURS 601 – 720)
* **Hours 601–630:** Gas Turbine Aerodynamics: Axial Compressor Staging, Shock Wave Ingestion, High-Pressure Turbine (HPT) Film Cooling Blades.
* **Hours 631–660:** Computational FEA & CFD: Von Mises Yield Stress Analysis, High-Cycle Fatigue (HCF) Life Prediction, Subsonic Aerodynamic Profiling.
* **Hours 661–720:** Capstone Sovereign Machine Build: Multi-disciplinary cohort synthesis—engineering, machining, assembling, and load-testing a functional turbomachine or automated robotic workcell.
