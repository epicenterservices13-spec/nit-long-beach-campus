# 🏛️ National Institute of Technology, Long Beach (NIT-LB)
### Interactive 3D Digital Twin & 720-Hour Sovereign Mechanical Engineering Campus

> *"Anchored on the Pacific Rim. Engineered for the deep space and maritime industrial corridors. Where mechanical theory meets titanium chips and fluid momentum."*

---

## 🌊 Overview
The **National Institute of Technology, Long Beach** (NIT-LB) is an interactive 3D digital campus and educational architecture modeling the premier coastal polytechnic institution of the Pacific Rim. Drawing structural inspiration from Long Beach's legendary maritime shipyards, aerospace rocketry hubs, and coastal civic pavilions, NIT-LB stands as the open-source flagship for 720 hours of rigorous hands-on mechanical tradecraft.

## 📐 Campus Architecture
- **The Central Engineering Pagoda:** A multi-tiered architectural landmark housing computational CFD superclusters, parametric CAD amphitheaters, and the dean's telemetry observatory.
- **Pacific Marine & Propulsion Hall:** Marine diesel engine test bays, cavitation water tunnels, and variable-pitch propeller dynamometers.
- **Orbital Aerospace Foundry:** 5-axis simultaneous trunnion milling centers, vacuum induction casting, and Inconel jet turbine diagnostics.
- **Coastal Breakwater Renewable Quad:** Wave-energy converters, industrial wind turbine nacelles, and closed-loop thermodynamic geothermal loops.

## 🕹️ Interactive 3D Simulation
Powered by a zero-dependency, self-contained Three.js WebGL engine featuring:
- Procedural ocean water shaders with dynamic wave crests.
- Architectural volumetric lighting and atmospheric marine layer fog.
- Raycasted building inspection displaying 720-hour curriculum modules, machine assets, and electrical power demands.
- Mobile and desktop orbit navigation.

## 📜 720-Hour Mechanical Curriculum
1. **Module 1 (Hours 001–120):** Foundations of Statics, Dynamics & Precision Metrology.
2. **Module 2 (Hours 121–240):** Subtractive Machining, G-Code & 5-Axis CNC Operations.
3. **Module 3 (Hours 241–360):** Thermodynamics, Heat Exchangers & Process Piping.
4. **Module 4 (Hours 361–480):** Metallurgy, Heat Treating & Structural Welding.
5. **Module 5 (Hours 481–600):** Industrial Hydraulics, Pneumatics & PLC Automation.
6. **Module 6 (Hours 601–720):** Gas Turbines, Aero Propulsion & Capstone Fabrication.
